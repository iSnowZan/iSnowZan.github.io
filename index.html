How I Build
01

Requirements before assumptions

Knowing what the customer actually wants beats building what seems correct. The client's goal steers the architecture — not the other way around.

02

Design for failure

Retries, idempotency, and observability aren't afterthoughts. Faults should be known before they cause havoc among users.

03

Keep systems boring

Simple architecture scales better than unnecessary complexity. Writing code that is easy for anyone to read is a responsibility, not an option.
